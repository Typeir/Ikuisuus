Aristophany is scored on one spell above. Its actual value is two spells at
once, so multiply its uptime by the second spell:
  one spell, no caster boon           71.5% uptime -> 0.72 spell-rounds
  one spell, Silent One (4 BP)        100.0% uptime -> 1.00 spell-rounds  (1.40x)
  two spells, Bilupine Aristophany (7 BP) 57.9% uptime -> 1.16 spell-rounds  (1.62x)

The disadvantage clause on Aristophany is doing real work: it costs about
14 points of uptime per spell, which is what stops 7 BP from simply doubling
the caster. Silent One buys 1.4x for 4 BP and buys it as certainty rather
than variance, which is the better deal at every table that fears losing a slot.

Nothing else in the bloodline chapter multiplies a caster's most limited
resource. Every other caster boon is additive.

roughly 60-70 damage against AC 18. One Filigree critical Seamline Reprise
puts out more than that, spread over the following 14 rounds, for one
Masterful Blow charge and no further action spend.

Counter-pressures the corpus already has:

    Medicine modifier + 1d4

    a boss and entirely real against a creature that has to keep fighting
