# Summon Aberration

_A writhing mass of alien flesh tears through reality at your command._

> **4th-level Conjuration**
>
> **Casting Time**: 1 Action
> **Range**: 90 feet
> **Components**: V, S, M
> **Duration**: 1 Hour (Concentration)

You call forth an aberrant spirit. The creature resembles a beholderkin with an AC of 11 + the spell's level, and it uses your proficiency bonus for its attack rolls and saving throws.

**At Higher Levels**: When you cast this spell at 5th level or higher, the creature's AC increases by 1 and its hit points increase by 10 for each slot level above 4th.
