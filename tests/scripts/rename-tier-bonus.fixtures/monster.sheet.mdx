## Traits

#### Titanic Godmass

The listed statistics represent Albedo's control nucleus.

#### Spellcasting

Albedo casts innate spells. Her spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 24, +16 to hit). The save DC equals 8 + her proficiency bonus + her Charisma modifier.

#### Cancerous Proliferation

When a creature within 60 feet takes damage, Albedo may use her reaction to add her proficiency bonus to the damage roll.
