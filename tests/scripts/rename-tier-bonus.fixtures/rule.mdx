# Checks and Rolls

## Proficiency Bonus

Whenever you make an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw using a skill, tool, or weapon with which you have proficiency, you add your **proficiency bonus** to the d20 roll.

Your proficiency bonus is determined by your character level, as shown in the Character Progression table.

### Proficiency Bonus by Level

| Level | Proficiency Bonus |
| ----- | ----------------- |
| 1-4   | +2                |
| 5-8   | +3                |
| 9-12  | +4                |
| 13-16 | +5                |
| 17-20 | +6                |

### Stacking Proficiency Bonus

Your proficiency bonus can't be added to a single die roll more than once. Occasionally, your proficiency bonus might be multiplied or divided (such as halved for familiarity, or doubled for expertise) before applying.

If a feature says "add double your proficiency bonus," calculate the numeric value first: `proficiencyBonus × 2`.
