# Empyrean

_Children of the sky, born from starlight and divine purpose._

The Empyreans are celestial-touched beings whose very blood sings with the music of the spheres.

## Core Features

| Feature            | Value          |
| ------------------ | -------------- |
| **Ability Scores** | +2 CHA, +1 WIS |
| **Speed**          | Walk: 30 ft.   |
| **Size**           | Medium         |

## Boons

<Collapsible title="Extended Reach">
### Extended Reach (Cost: 3 BP)

Your celestial heritage manifests physically. The reach of your melee attacks increases by 5 feet. You may use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Additionally, when you make an opportunity attack, you may add your proficiency bonus to the damage.

</Collapsible>

<Collapsible title="Starborn Resilience">
### Starborn Resilience (Cost: 4 BP)

You gain temporary hit points equal to twice your proficiency bonus whenever you finish a short rest.

</Collapsible>
