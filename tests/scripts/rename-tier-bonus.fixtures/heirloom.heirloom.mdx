# Arms of the Black One

_Rarity: Legendary (requires attunement)_

_Gauntlets_

These gauntlets were forged from the bones of the Black One itself.

**Properties**: +3 to attack rolls
**Damage**: 2d8 bludgeoning
**Damage Type**: Bludgeoning

While attuned, you gain the following benefits:

- Your unarmed strikes deal an additional 1d8 necrotic damage.
- You may add your proficiency bonus to initiative rolls.
- When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points, you regain hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.
